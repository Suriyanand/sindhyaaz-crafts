Frequently Asked Questions ❓

Everything you need to know about shopping with Sindhyaaz Crafts

1. How do I personalize gifts with names or photos? ▼
After selecting any customizable gift (like name necklaces, LED photo frames, mugs), simply place your order on our site, or click the "Order via WhatsApp" button. Our design artisan will directly connect with you on WhatsApp to collect your high-resolution photos, names, and customized messages for approval before crafting!
2. How long will delivery take across India? ▼
Non-custom items ship within 24 hours. Handcrafted personalized items require 24-48 hours for artisanal crafting and setting. Express courier transit takes 3 to 5 business days anywhere in India. Live tracking links are shared upon dispatch!
3. What payment methods do you accept? Is COD available? ▼
Yes! We support Cash on Delivery (COD) across all major Indian pincodes. We also accept instant UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards, and NetBanking.
4. What if my custom item arrives damaged? ▼
We package all gifts with high-grade multi-layer shockproof bubble cushioning. In the rare case of transit damage, just send an unboxing video to our WhatsApp (+91 8098587377) within 48 hours and we will ship an instant 100% free replacement!
5. Do you handle bulk return gifts for weddings & birthdays? ▼
Yes! We specialize in wholesale bulk orders for return gifts, baby showers, weddings, and corporate events with custom branding and wholesale discounts. Chat with us on WhatsApp for bulk catalogs and custom pricing.
